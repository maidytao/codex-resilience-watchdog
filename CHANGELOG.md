## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-27

### Added

- Persistent task, checkpoint, heartbeat, lease, incident, and circuit state.
- Read-only Codex log observation and declared result reconciliation.
- Closed automatic replay policy for read-only repeatable operations.
- Two-recovery, one-restart, and repeated-fault circuit limits.
- Verified Codex Desktop restart and fixed-argument read-only resume backend.
- Single-instance per-user daemon, asynchronous notifications, and bounded logs.
- Codex skill, protocol reference, reversible installer, and safe uninstaller.
- English and Simplified Chinese usage documentation.
